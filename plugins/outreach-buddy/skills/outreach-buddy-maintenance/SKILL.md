          # Outreach Buddy Maintenance

          ## Source of truth

          All step content lives in the bundled file stappenplan.md at the plugin root (../../stappenplan.md relative to this skill folder). Read Step 17 (Onderhoud) from it fresh with the Read tool.

          ## Flow

          1. Walk through Step 17's checklist with the creator, one item at a time: new brands or collaborations to add to the about me document, rate changes, frequency or cadence adjustments, and tone-of-voice refinements based on what has landed well or poorly recently.
          2. For anything the creator wants changed, read the relevant existing document from their Project, make the edit, and write the updated version back. Don't ask them to redo a whole document for a small change.
          3. If the creator wants to shift niche focus, remind them they can do that any time by saying "vanaf nu deze niches: [...]" - handled live by their ideale klant & niche document.
          4. If a scheduled task feels off (wrong time, too frequent, wrong notification channel), tell them they can adjust or stop it by describing the change in plain language.
          5. Close with a one-line summary of what changed during this check-in.
